- [ ] Send the proposal to Forbrukerrådet, Finanstilsynet, the Ministry of Justice and Storting committees

## Ground rules

- No personal data about real debtors, ever.
- Every claim backed by a source.
- The goal is fair collection, not cancelling debts.
