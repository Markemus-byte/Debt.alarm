# Code of conduct / Adferdsregler

Many people here have been through hard things: illness, debt, grief. Treat everyone with respect.

Mange her har opplevd tunge ting: sykdom, gjeld, sorg. Behandle alle med respekt.

## Expected / Forventet

- Be kind and patient, especially with people sharing their own experiences.
- Criticise ideas, not people.
- Back factual claims with sources.

## Not accepted / Ikke akseptert

- Harassment, insults or threats.
- Posting personal data about anyone (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Naming or shaming individual employees of collection companies or public agencies.
- Spam or advertising.

## Enforcement / Håndheving

The maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) may edit or remove content and block people who break these rules. Report problems by contacting a maintainer on GitHub.
