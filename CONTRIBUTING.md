# Contributing / Bidra

Thank you for wanting to help. The foundation is in place (see [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md)), and the project is now open for stories, knowledge, sources and code.

Takk for at du vil hjelpe. Grunnmuren er på plass, og prosjektet er nå åpent for historier, kunnskap, kilder og kode.

## What we need / Hva vi trenger

- **Experiences**: how debt collection affected you or someone close to you
- **Knowledge**: law, debt advice, health services, NAV, the bailiff system
- **Sources**: reports, statistics, news articles, court decisions
- **Ideas and code**: for the early warning system

## Privacy rules (important) / Personvern (viktig)

- **Never post names, national ID numbers, addresses, case numbers or health details** about yourself or anyone else.
- Anonymise stories: change details that could identify a person.
- Do not post private letters or documents from collection firms without removing all personal data.
- Content that breaks these rules will be removed.
- Before sharing a story, read the guide [Sharing experiences safely](docs/del-trygt.md).

**Aldri publiser navn, fødselsnummer, adresser, saksnumre eller helseopplysninger** om deg selv eller andre. Les [guiden for trygg deling](docs/del-trygt.md) før du deler en historie.

## How to contribute

1. **Just want to share a thought?** Start a conversation under Discussions.
2. **A story, idea or source?** Open an issue using one of the templates.
3. **A change to documents or code?** Fork the repository and open a pull request with a short description. The founder reviews and decides what is merged (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).
4. Back factual claims with a link to a source.

Be respectful. Many people here have been through hard things. See the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
