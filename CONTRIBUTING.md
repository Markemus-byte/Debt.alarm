# Contributing / Bidra

Thank you for wanting to help. The project is still in its foundation phase, and contributions will open fully in Phase 2 (see [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md)). Until then, issues and suggestions are welcome.

Takk for at du vil hjelpe. Prosjektet er fortsatt i grunnmursfasen, og bidrag åpnes fullt i fase 2. Inntil da tar vi gjerne imot forslag.

## What we need / Hva vi trenger

- **Experiences**: how debt collection affected you or someone close to you
- **Knowledge**: law, debt advice, health services, NAV, the bailiff system
- **Sources**: reports, statistics, news articles, court decisions
- **Ideas and code**: for the early warning system

## Privacy rules (important) / Personvern (viktig)

- **Never post names, national ID numbers, addresses, case numbers or health details** about yourself or anyone else.
- Anonymise stories: change details that could identify a person.
- Do not post private letters or documents from collection firms without removing all personal data.
- Content that breaks these rules will be removed.

**Aldri publiser navn, fødselsnummer, adresser, saksnumre eller helseopplysninger** om deg selv eller andre.

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue using one of the templates (story, idea or source).
2. For changes to documents or code, open a pull request with a short description.
3. Back factual claims with a link to a source.

Be respectful. Many people here have been through hard things.
